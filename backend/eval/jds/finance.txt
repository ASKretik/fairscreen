Financial Analyst

About the role
Our finance team supports a growing consumer-goods business with annual revenue of about $120M. The Financial Analyst will build budgets and forecasts, analyse performance against plan, and give business leaders the numbers they need to make decisions.

What you will do
- Prepare the annual budget and monthly rolling forecasts with business-unit owners.
- Perform monthly variance analysis (actual vs. budget vs. forecast) and explain the drivers.
- Build and maintain financial models for pricing, capital expenditure and new initiatives.
- Support month-end close by preparing reconciliations and management reports.
- Produce dashboards and presentations for senior management.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics or a related field.
- 2+ years of experience in financial analysis, FP&A, accounting or a related role.
- Advanced Microsoft Excel skills (pivot tables, lookups, financial modelling).
- Experience with budgeting, forecasting and variance analysis.
- Working knowledge of GAAP and financial statements.

Nice to have
- Progress towards CPA or CFA.
- Experience with an ERP system such as SAP, Oracle or NetSuite.
- Experience with SQL, Power BI or Tableau.
- Experience in a manufacturing or consumer-goods company.

Good communication skills and attention to detail will help you succeed in this role.
