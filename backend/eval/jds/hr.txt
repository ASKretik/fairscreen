HR Generalist

About the role
We are a 400-employee healthcare services company hiring an HR Generalist to support managers and employees across three sites. You will handle the full employee lifecycle, from hiring to exit, and help us build fair and consistent HR processes.

What you will do
- Manage end-to-end recruitment for hourly and salaried roles: postings, screening, interviews and offers.
- Run onboarding and offboarding, including documentation and HRIS updates.
- Advise managers on employee relations issues, performance management and investigations.
- Administer benefits enrolment and answer employee questions on benefits and leave.
- Keep policies and practices compliant with employment law and maintain accurate employee records.
- Coordinate training programmes and support annual performance reviews.

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience as an HR Generalist or in a similar HR role.
- Experience with full-cycle recruitment and onboarding.
- Experience handling employee relations matters.
- Working knowledge of US employment law (e.g. FLSA, FMLA, ADA, EEO).
- Experience using an HRIS such as Workday, ADP or BambooHR.
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration or a related field.

Nice to have
- SHRM-CP or PHR certification.
- Experience with benefits administration or payroll processing.
- Experience designing or delivering training programmes.
- Experience in a healthcare setting.

We are looking for someone approachable, organised and discreet.
