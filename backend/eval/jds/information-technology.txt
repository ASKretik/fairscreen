IT Systems Administrator

About the role
We are a 250-person logistics company looking for an IT Systems Administrator to keep our offices, warehouse and remote staff running. You will own our Windows server estate, user accounts, network and end-user support, and work with a small IT team of three.

What you will do
- Administer Windows Server, Active Directory, Group Policy, DNS and DHCP.
- Manage Microsoft 365 (Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint) and user onboarding and offboarding.
- Maintain the LAN/WAN, firewalls, switches, Wi-Fi and VPN; troubleshoot connectivity issues.
- Run our VMware virtual environment, backups and disaster-recovery tests.
- Act as the escalation point for help-desk tickets and keep our ticketing system and documentation up to date.
- Patch and secure servers and endpoints; support security audits.

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience in systems administration or IT support.
- Hands-on experience administering Windows Server and Active Directory.
- Solid understanding of networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, VLANs).
- Experience supporting end users and resolving hardware and software issues.
- Experience with virtualization (VMware or Hyper-V).
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent experience.

Nice to have
- CompTIA A+, Network+ or Security+, or a Microsoft certification.
- Scripting with PowerShell to automate admin tasks.
- Experience with cloud platforms (Azure or AWS).
- Familiarity with ITIL practices.

We value clear communication and a calm approach to fixing problems under pressure.
